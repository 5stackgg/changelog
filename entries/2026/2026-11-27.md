# Friday, November 27, 2026

Today's update lets admins decide which kinds of match count toward ELO, and shows why a match is unrated.

## Matchmaking
- **Fixed:** Large parties no longer push the seat row past the edge of the mode tiles on the Play page. Beyond two extra seats, the tile now shows a count.

## Matches
- **New:** Match organizers can turn ELO on or off for a custom or scrim match from the match page's Settings tab. It locks once the map veto starts, and everyone can see in the match options whether the match is rated.

## Tournaments
- **Improved:** ELO is now one of the tournament's options, and organizers can turn it off in the creation wizard. It locks once a tournament match reaches the map veto, and a tournament created by a player starts unrated until a tournament organizer turns ELO on.

## Stats & Leaderboards
- **New:** Admins choose which kinds of match are rated from Settings › Application › ELO. The choice is made separately for online and LAN play and covers matchmaking, full parties, drafts, scrims, custom matches and tournaments.
- **New:** Unrated matches show an amber icon that explains why. It appears on match lists, profile match history, the match header, draft lobbies and the full-party matchmaking line.
